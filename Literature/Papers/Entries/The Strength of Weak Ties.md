## Reading Notes
`=` Strong tie
`-` Weak tie

- Several studies observe: if `A = B ∧ A = C ⇒ A = C`
- a tie is a bride if its the only (direct and indirect) path between A and B
- briding pathes are important for large network structure as they are the only way that information can travel between A and his friends and B and his friends
- bridging ties are probably not strong as that would imply redundant strong connections. ⇒ "strength of weak ties"
- all bridges are weak ties
- strict bridge rarely present globaly in big networks. but bridging function localy exists
- only indirect connections of degree **n** are viable for communication
- "local bridge of degree n"
- when randomly removing any tie the "damage" dont to transmision probabilites is higher when removing weak ties
