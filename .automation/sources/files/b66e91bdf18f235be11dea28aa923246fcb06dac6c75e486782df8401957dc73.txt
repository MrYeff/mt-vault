A platform-independent field experiment demonstrates that reranking content expressing antidemocratic attitudes and partisan animosity in social media feeds
alters affective polarization. A browser extension reranked participants’ social media feeds by reducing or increasing exposure to posts expressing AAPA, which in turn led to
corresponding decreases or increases in affective polarization.

emotions. Then, they were randomly assigned to one of the two parallel experiments, reduced or increased AAPA content exposure, and further randomized into treatment
and control groups. After 10 days, they completed a post-experiment survey. (B) Timeline of the experiment. During the first 3 days, no intervention was applied to measure

periodically shown throughout the baseline and the intervention period. (C) The interventions down-­or up-­ranked AAPA posts in the participants’ feeds depending on their
treatment assignment.
